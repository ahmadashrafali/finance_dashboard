Foundry — the idea pipeline
The law: Decision on intake · armed not advised · ≤10 active · 4-week clocks · one deferral max · reject prices not companies · displacement required · every pass scored.
How Foundry works
▸
1 · Intake. Every idea enters the moment it appears — Express (a buy-now call from a trusted channel) or Standard (a research idea). Decision on intake: nothing sits in a gray pile.
2 · Arm. BUY and PASS conditions are written on day one. Armed, not advised — the level decides, not the mood.
3 · Clock. Standard runs a 4-week DEADLINE (one deferral max); Express vets same-day with a ≤24h verdict. The clock kills stalling — it never delays buying.
4 · Verdict. Buy (dossier before first tranche) · Scout (~$6K starter with a written kill line) · Bench (quality at the wrong price → standing alert) · Pass (scored, never re-argued).
5 · Graduate or exit. A scout has 6 months: graduate with a displacement case — naming which of the 20 it replaces — or exit by its rules.
Example: Saif says buy DUOL now. You tap Express intake → we vet same-day (redlines + 5-factor + price sanity) → verdict in 24h → PASS arms a ~$6K scout starter with a written kill line → 6 months later DUOL either graduates (names which of the 20 it replaces) or exits by its rules. If instead it's quality-but-expensive, it goes to the Bench at the price it WOULD be a buy — and that alert firing = automatic re-entry.
⚡ EXPRESS
Buy-now calls from trusted channels: same-day vet, ≤24h verdict, passes land as SCOUT starters.
🔬 STANDARD
Research ideas: BUY/PASS conditions written on day one, 4-week clock as DEADLINE — a candidate can be armed the same day it enters. The clock kills stalling, it never delays buying.
